Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel up to 24 hours before your scheduled time for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a fee.

Do memberships auto-renew?

Yes, monthly memberships auto-renew unless cancelled. You can cancel anytime from your account settings.

Can I upgrade my membership?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your membership at any time. The price difference will be prorated for the remaining period.

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees! The prices shown are exactly what you'll pay. Equipment rental and other services are clearly priced.
