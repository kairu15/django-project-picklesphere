FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book a court?

You can easily book a court through our online booking system. Create an account, select your preferred date and time, and complete the payment.

Can I rent equipment?

Yes! We offer paddle and ball rentals at affordable rates. Members enjoy free equipment rental as part of their membership benefits.

Do you offer coaching?

Absolutely! We have certified pickleball coaches available for private and group lessons. Contact us to schedule a session.

How do I join tournaments?

Tournament registration is done through our website. Navigate to the Tournaments page, select an event, and click Register to join.
