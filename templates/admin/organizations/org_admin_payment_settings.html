How It Works
  1. A player books one of your courts and reaches checkout.
  2. They instantly see {{ organization.name }}'s payment details — your number, account name, QR code and instructions.
  3. They send the payment and upload proof (reference number + screenshot).
  4. Your staff verifies the payment and confirms the reservation.
